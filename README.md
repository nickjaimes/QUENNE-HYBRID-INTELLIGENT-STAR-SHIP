## Overview

The **QUENNE Hybrid Intelligent Star Ship (Q-HISS)** is a conceptual next-generation deep-space platform designed using the **QUENNE Stacked Intelligence Architecture**.

Q-HISS introduces **cognitive spacecraft**, where intelligence is embedded into the spacecraft core, enabling autonomous multi-year missions while preserving human governance.

The platform integrates:

• Photonic processing  
• Neuromorphic reflex intelligence  
• AI mission reasoning  
• Quantum-assisted optimization  
• Human governance & mission authority  

Q-HISS extends intelligence infrastructure from Earth and orbit into **deep-space operations**.
